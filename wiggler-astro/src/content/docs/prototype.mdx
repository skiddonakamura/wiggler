## 1. Introduction to the Weapon

Switch Axe rewards deliberate positioning, careful gauge management, and knowing when to exchange safety for damage. Start with one stable loop; add options as the matchup becomes familiar.

### Who this is for

- **Intermediate:** wants a reliable endgame route and a clear baseline.
- **Advanced:** can adapt positioning and uptime to a monster’s openings.

## 3. Mechanics and Numbers

### Damage model

$$
\text{Raw damage} = ((\text{Base Raw} \times \text{modifiers}) + \text{flat raw})
\times \text{sharpness} \times \frac{\text{HZV}}{100} \times \frac{\text{MV}}{100}
$$

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Raw | Weapon attack before elemental damage. |
| MV | Motion value: how much of the attack stat a move uses. |
| HZV | Hitzone value: the monster part’s damage multiplier. |

## 4. Endgame Build Guide

<BuildCard title="The adaptable baseline" tier="Meta" audience="Advanced" calculator="https://www.mathhunter.ca/">

#### Requirements

- A suitable rarity-8 weapon and a level-appropriate charm.
- Match elemental attack to the weapon element.

#### Gameplan

Use Rising Slash for dependable sustained damage. Switch to Full Release when the monster presents a long, predictable opening.

#### Comfort options

Stun Resistance, Speed Eating, and Shockproof for multiplayer are safe additions.

</BuildCard>
