## 1. When to use Progression Builds

This progression Guide is aimed at players who are new to the franchise, feel overwhelmed by all the options and choices the game offers, and those who are just looking for a refresher or need some pointers for what to look out for during progression.

The Sets themselves aim to support you, from the start of the game all the way until the Endgame. From there, you should be in a great spot to follow the Endgame sets, accessible through the sidemenu or the buttons at the end of the page.

Do note that the entirety of this guide consists mainly of Spoilers! Proceed at your own discretion.

## 2. Story Progression Guide

### 2.1 Chapter 1

Monster Hunter Wilds Progression has an extremely railroaded Low Rank Story, that consists of a total of 20 hunts. Most of the sets don't really do a whole lot of anything, usually providing, at best, a higher defense stat.

Hope Hammer II is upgradable from the moment you unlock upgrading, Gemma provides the mats for it. Most hunts are not very challenging, and the majority of sets are strictly not worth it.

![Wilds Chapter 1 Hammer Set](/wilds/progression/sets/hammer1.webp)

Once you unlock the Doshaguma hunt, its set becomes available. The 4 Piece Set bonus it gives grants +25 Attack after a successful offset. If you're good with those, it's the first actual offensive Set available, but overall not worth the effort.

This should easily bring you into Chapter 2.

### 2.2 Chapter 2

After the Rey hunt in early chapter 2, Hope Hammer 3 becomes craftable as an upgrade. 2 hunts later, Hirabami is unlocked.

As previously noted, the main purpose of this set is having more than 5 defense.

![Wilds Chapter 2 Hammer Set](/wilds/progression/sets/hammer2.webp)

### 2.3 Chapter 3

The second hunt in Chapter 3, and we finally unlock a set with actual skills on it.

Guardian Rathalos Feet and Arms are recommended to craft here. The helmet is good too, but requires a plate to make. If you don't have that, it's completely fine to skip.

After a bit of story, Guardian Ebony is unlocked, which is a set favoured by many people. The Coil in particular is easy to make and provides 1 level of Burst, which is better than most other pieces at this point in the game.

Immediately after, Xu Wu is unlocked, which has one of the best craftable Hammers in the game. It's a strong consideration to make it.

![Wilds Final Low Rank Hammer Set](/wilds/progression/sets/hammer3.webp)

## 3. Early Post-Game Progression Guide

### 3.1 Chapter 4

After the Junky chicken hunt, we leave the railroad and finally get to do our thing. Of the monsters that now unlock, the biggest ones are the two Raths.

Los comes with built-in Critical Boost 3, which is really nice for low Decoration amount sets, and will be the recommended from here on out. Make sure you hunt a Chatacabra and Rathian, as those materials are required to craft the Weakness Exploit I charm.

The resulting set will last you for pretty much the rest of the game.

![Wilds Early High Rank Hammer Set](/wilds/progression/sets/hammer4.webp)

From this point onward, the recommended way to progress is doing Side Quests. They unlock the "farms", some new systems like random villagers inviting you to dinner, the Power and Armor charm for some free Raw, aswell as some decoration melding options. After doing them, you should be well on your way to Hunter Rank 40, which is the current final monster unlock of the game.

## 4. Late Post-Game Progression Guide

### 4.1 HR30+

This set is basically 1 piece off the Meta set. This can and will take you through all content with ease, and all of the pieces made here are GOOD and used in numerous sets, so don't fear about grinding them.

I understand you might not have all of these decos, but the set is still GREAT without them, so just make it and don't worry about anything.

![Wilds HR30 Hammer Set](/wilds/progression/sets/hammer5.webp)

After you defeat Arkveld and finish HR 40, swap over to the Meta guide for your next builds.
