## 2. Build Assumptions

The following sets assume you have knowledge of your moveset options, understand the weapon's kit and matchups, have reached endgame content, and have access to higher-tier decoration quests.

### 2.1 Skill Priority

**Offensive Armor Skills (Equal Priority - Level 1):**

- Counterstrike 3 (free 25 Raw for 45 seconds when proccing on Hyper Armor/Counter)
- Maximum Might 3 (affinity on armor; all important Swaxe moves can crit)
- Agitator OR Weakness Exploit 5 (affinity source)

**Priority Level 2:**

- Burst 1 (10 Raw at Stage 2)
- 2-Piece Gore + Antivirus 3 OR Latent Power + 2-Piece Rey

### 2.1B Weapon Skill Priority

| Importance | Skill | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Power Prolonger 3 | Improves Amp uptime for more Full Release Slashes |
| 1 | Critical Boost | Undisputedly the best Skill in the Game |
| 2 | Handicraft | Only useful as secondary on Power Prolonger decoration |
| 3 | Rapid Morph | Weaker than Rise/Sunbreak; enables alternative playstyle |
| -9999 | Focus | [It's really bad](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L8omNniTOk8) |

### 2.2 Other Considerations

**Food Skills:**

- First slot: Meat base grants +2 Raw
- Second slot: Caprice Meal (Hi) provides +15 Raw in 10-second intervals, or Tumbler Meal for dodge iframes
- Third slot: Moxie Meal prevents one cart (varies by set/location)

**Out-of-Quest Setup:**

Items consumed in safe areas persist into quests. Using Mushroomancer + Item Prolonger accelerates buff farming through mushrooms and seeds.

**Palico:**

Current consensus recommends Para weapon or leaving it at home. Para is preferred to avoid interfering with Agitator. Palicos unlock abilities via side quests (revive on cart, spawn Vigor Wasps, steal materials, buff attack). Maximum level: 30.

### 2.3 Preamble: Artian Weapons

The current meta revolves around GogArtian Weapons.

**Crafting Requirements:**

Materials from HR41+ Tempered Investigations. Creating a Switch Axe requires 2x Blade + 1x Device from Gemma's forge menu.

**Part Selection:**

Each part has Element/Status with Attack or Affinity. Using two identical Element/Status creates that element weapon; three identical parts boosts the value. Current highest-tier Artians have 190 Raw, 5% Affinity, 20 White Sharpness, Dragon Phial, and 3-3-3 slots.

**Infusion Mechanics:**

Each part's infusion equals +5 to its associated stat. Example: 2 Attack + 1 Affinity = +10 Attack, +5% Affinity.

**Reinforcement:**

Ten Oricalcite per roll (50 ore = 5 rolls). Each reinforcement adds one of four possible bonuses:

- +5% Affinity
- +3 Element/Status
- +5 Raw
- +30 Sharpness units

Recommended target: +60 Sharpness (2 rolls), remaining into Attack for 205-210 total Attack, 80 White Sharpness, Power Phial, 8(0) Para. Dismantling returns Ore but loses crafting parts.

### 2.4 Preamble: GogArtian Weapons

GogArtian weapons upgrade from Artian weapons via smithy menu (Forge/Upgrade > Upgrade to Gogma Artian).

**Upgrade Focus Options:**

| Focus Type | Attack | Affinity | White Sharpness | Phial Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Attack Focus | +10 | -15 | — | Power |
| Affinity Focus | -10 | +10 | -10 | Element |
| Element Focus | — | -5 | — | Element (+4 element) |

**Reinforcement Bonuses:**

| Category | Tier I | Tier II | Tier III | EX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Attack | 5 | 6 | 9 | 12 |
| Affinity | 5 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| Element | 3 | 5 | N/A | 8 |
| Sharpness | 30 | N/A | N/A | 50 |

Maximum two EX-tier reinforcements per category. Recommended rolls for Switch Axe: 2x Sharpness EX, 2x Attack EX, 1x Affinity EX.

## 3. Matchup Chart

![Switch Axe Matchup Chart](/wilds/endgame/sets/switch_axe_matchup.webp)

The matchup chart compares the Pinnacle (Power Phial) and Ele Phial sets across Monster Hunter Wilds monsters. Element and Raw have different playstyles — Element Phial builds should target elementally weak hitzones like glue, while Power Phial builds prioritize raw damage output through sustained Rising Slash loops.

Key notes:

- **Blangonga:** Won by Ele Phial exclusively on the Torso hitzone
- **Guardian Rathalos:** Won by Ele Phial exclusively on the Feet hitzone
- **Gogmazios:** Can use Power Phial with Fire element and Mind's Eye 3 for Phase 1 optimization
- **Nerscylla:** Uncalculated due to insufficient HP variation affecting hitzone analysis
- Additional monsters have element advantages on specific body parts (heads, necks, arms)

Elemental advantages on non-optimal hitzones are not the best target hitzones but are included due to multiplayer situations where positioning is less controllable.

## 4. Meta Builds

### 4.1 Gear Requirements

HR100 is the minimum requirement, though full optimization requires additional grinding for decorations and materials. Every non-Level 3 decoration becomes target-meldable at HR100, ensuring players won't be completely locked out of sets even with bad luck.

### 4.2 The Pinnacle — Power Phial

![The Pinnacle Set](/wilds/endgame/sets/switch_axe4.2.webp)

**Primary Combo:** Rising Slash (RISING SLASH)

**Requirements:**

- Specific pictured weapon only
- Rarity 8 Charm with Attack Boost 3 and either Burst 1 or Agitator 1
- Two level 1 armor decoration slots

This build emphasizes Rising Slash as the primary damage loop. While mathematically optimal for sustained DPS, Full Release Slashes remain valuable for burst situations. Players should avoid rolling frequently to maintain Counterstrike uptime through counters and Latent Power through Full Release usage.

Requires matching elemental versions — five different weapons total, one per element. The guide recommends against comfort skills: get better at not having comfort Skills.

**Recommended Comfort Skills (if needed):** Shockproof 1, Stun Resistance 3, Quick Sheathe 3, Speed Eating 3

### 4.3 Ele Phial

![Ele Phial Set](/wilds/endgame/sets/switch_axe4.3.webp)

**Primary Combo:** Triple Slash

**Requirements:**

- Pictured weapon (one per element)
- Power Prolonger 3 Charm with Level 2 slot
- One level of Burst, Latent Power, or Agitator

This build targets elementally weak hitzones like glue using Triple Slash. Element Phials outperform the Pinnacle on monsters marked red in the matchup chart. Use Full Release for sustained versus burst damage depending on positioning.

Counterstrike juggling uses Offsets rather than pure counters, though this provides minimal impact compared to Latent Power juggling.

**Recommended Comfort Skills:** Shockproof 1, Stun Resistance 3, Quick Sheathe 3, Speed Eating 3

### 4.4 Convert Element: Element Phial

![Convert Element Set](/wilds/endgame/sets/switch_axe4.4.webp)

**Primary Combo:** Triple Slash with Convert Element

**Requirements:**

- Pictured weapon (one per element)
- Charm with Max Might 2, Level 2 slot, ideally Critical Element 2+
- Antipara deco denotes empty Level 1 charm slot

This build activates on any elemental damage taken, synergizing well with counter gameplay. The skill maintains 57% maximum uptime (120s active / 210s cycle), and the explosion effect scales with raw damage while requiring elemental damage capability.

This set exists only and exclusively for sake of completeness, not as a general recommendation — matchup victories require perfect conditions that typically won't occur in average hunts.

**Recommended Comfort Skills:** Stun Resistance 3, Shockproof, Bleed Resistance

### 4.5 Budget: Resentment Gore

![Budget Resentment Gore Set](/wilds/endgame/sets/switch_axe4.5.webp)

**Primary Combo:** Rising Slash with Power Phial

**Requirements:**

- Gogma Artian weapon with Gore Magala's Tyranny
- Power Phial with Attack Focus
- 2x Sharpness, 2x Attack, 1x Affinity reinforcements (ideally EX tier)

Arkveld doesn't take much element damage; use alternative status and avoid Dragonblight.

Rising Slash remains the primary loop with Power Phials. The Resentment mechanic provides consistent bonus damage without relying on environmental procs. Like all Power Phial sets, Triple Slash underperforms, but Full Release maintains value for burst scenarios.

**Recommended Comfort Skills:** Stun Resistance, Speed Eating, Shockproof 1 (multiplayer), Tremor Resistance, Aquatic Mobility 1, Quick Sheathe, Adaptability 1

**Upgrade Path:** The baseline provides Critical Boost 5, but optimization suggests reducing to Critical Boost 3 while adding Attack Boost 5, rolling Guts on the weapon, incorporating Rey 2-Piece for consistency, and adding Flayer 1 or Adrenaline Rush 2.

[Baseline Set on wiki-db](https://mhwilds.wiki-db.com/sim/?hl=en#skills=Black%20Eclipse%20I%2CAgitator%20Lv5%2CCounterstrike%20Lv3%2CMaximum%20Might%20Lv3%2CAntivirus%20Lv3%2CBurst%20Lv1%2CResentment%20Lv5%2CLatent%20Power%20Lv3%2CGuts%20\(Tenacity\)%2CBad%20Blood%20I&s=1&e=1&v=10&g=13&w=&ws=&d=0&rf=-100&rw=-100&rt=-100&ri=-100&rd=-100&l=200&wss=Gore%20Magala's%20Tyranny)

### 4.6 Budget: Element Phial

![Budget Element Phial Set](/wilds/endgame/sets/switch_axe4.6.webp)

**Primary Combo:** Triple Slash with Element Phial

**Requirements:**

- Gogma Artian weapon with Gore Magala's Tyranny
- Element Phial with Element Focus
- 2x Sharpness, 3x Element reinforcements (ideally 2x Sharpness EX, 2x Element EX, 1x Element II)

Triple Slash application strategy mirrors the non-budget Element Phial set, with players sticking to weak hitzones. The matchup chart (Section 3) determines which monsters favor this build over the Pinnacle.

**Recommended Comfort Skills:** Stun Resistance, Speed Eating, Shockproof 1 (multiplayer), Tremor Resistance, Aquatic Mobility 1, Quick Sheathe, Adaptability 1

**Upgrade Path:** Optimization involves increasing Critical Element from 1 to 3, boosting Critical Boost from 3 to 5, adding Guts to the weapon, incorporating more Coalescence and Latent Power, and potentially adding Convert Element.

### 4.7 Budget: E-Con Power Phial

![Budget E-Con Power Phial Set](/wilds/endgame/sets/switch_axe4.7.webp)

**Primary Combo:** Rising Slash with Convert Element

**Requirements:**

- Gogma Artian weapon with Gore Magala's Tyranny
- Power Phial with Attack Focus
- 2x Sharpness, 2x Attack, 1x Affinity reinforcements (ideally EX tier)
- Element Attack option as secondary on Power Prolonger 3

Arkveld doesn't take much element damage; use alternative status and avoid Dragonblight.

This hybrid approach combines Power Phial Rising Slash gameplay with Convert Element's explosion mechanics. Convert Element activates on any elemental damage received, providing proc-based damage during its 57% uptime window.

This exists for sake of completeness with perfect conditions caveats. Power Phials will fall off earlier than Element Phials due to budget constraints on individual armor pieces, with future content potentially changing this calculus.

**Recommended Comfort Skills:** Stun Resistance, Speed Eating, Shockproof 1 (multiplayer), Tremor Resistance, Aquatic Mobility 1, Quick Sheathe, Adaptability 1

## 7. Title Update Changes

![Title Update History](/wilds/endgame/sets/switch_axe7.b.webp)

### Title Update 4 (Latest)

Rising Slash buffed substantially (33→40 MV overhead; 42→58 right slash; 48→62 left slash), pushing Rising Loop to ~60 MV/s while amped. Artian upgrades now grant Power Phials with elemental properties. Gogmazios introduced with 410k HP and multiple health locks as primary grind target. Performance optimizations implemented but results vary.

### Title Update 3

Added Omega/savage variants and Nu Udra (Arch-Tempered). Omega 2-Piece offers elemental Resentment variant; 4-Piece unlocks The Blackest Night action (shields against death, enables health recovery). Nu Udra chest provides Counterstrike 2 and dual 3-slots but lacks competitive pieces elsewhere.

### Title Update 2

Seregios and Lagiacrus introduced. Lagiacrus features built-in Convert Thunder Resistance boosting element attack. Phial ticks no longer magnetize to hitzone hits; Max Might maintenance simplified. Full Release received travel distance increase and morph MV buffs (+8 per hit). Bridging Slash improved by 10 MV.

### Title Update 1

Mizutsune and Zoh Shia rematch added. Zoh's chest became meta-relevant efficiency piece. Arch-Tempered Rey Dau introduced "Guts" skill providing ~5% attack. Max Might maintenance eased, largely removing 2-piece Fulgur Anja from meta.

### OBT to Release

Switch Axe received dramatic number increases — damage loop jumped from ~160 MV to 506 MV; Rising Slash combo at 123 MV. Element and Raw differentiated as distinct playstyles for first time.

## 8. FAQ

**Rapid Morph?**
Alternative playstyle but not meta-viable due to damage loss and buff-state juggling.

**Focus?**
Doesn't really do anything, competing against Critical Boost and Power Prolonger.

**Partbreaker?**
Currently doesn't increase Focus Strike damage; only boosts wound break damage (~30).

**Amp Duration?**
45 seconds base; Power Prolonger 3 extends to 90 seconds.

**Power Axe?**
+10 Raw in Axe Mode only.

**Power Charm?**
+6 Raw.

**Offset?**
Operates on a scaling threshold; topple inconsistency is explained by the threshold resetting after each successful proc.

**Scorcher?**
Procs extra fire damage hit (~10/30 based chance); scales with Burst, Coalescence, Fire Attack — not weapon attack stat. [Video explanation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4F1w3r6xgc)

**Frenzy Mantle?**
Drains health, procs bonus hits on specific moves, grants +10 Affinity and +5% Raw when invisible gauge fills.

**Nourishing Extract?**
Double Guardian investigations provide abundance.

**Gore 4-Piece?**
2-Piece infects with frenzy; 4-Piece adds 10 Raw (15 total after clearing infection).
