## Basic Controls

The Greatsword is a powerful weapon with slow, devastating attacks. Master these basic controls:

- **Draw Attack:** <ControlDisplay control="X" />
- **Overhead Slash:** <ControlDisplay control="X" />
- **Charged Slash:** Hold <ControlDisplay control="X" />
- **Tackle:** <ControlDisplay control="B" />
- **Guard:** <ControlDisplay control="ZR" />

## Advanced Techniques

- **True Charged Slash:** After landing a Charged Slash, hold <ControlDisplay control="X" /> again for maximum damage
- **Shoulder Tackle into TCS:** During any charge, press <ControlDisplay control="B" /> to tackle through attacks and maintain your combo
